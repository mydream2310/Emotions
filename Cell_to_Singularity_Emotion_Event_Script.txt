THE CORE DESIGN IDEA (one paragraph, for the impatient reader)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The tree descends. It begins at the surface — the raw weather of nerve
signal, brain circuit, and molecule (Sensations). At the end of that
biological spine, the two axes of raw feeling converge, and the first thing
they produce is THE OPEN SMILE: the infant's first social smile, the moment
feeling first becomes a message sent to another mind. That smile opens the
expression economy (Expressions) — the face's grammar, then the eight great
basic-emotion families fanning like petals, capped by Darwin's book. Then
the tree dives: six strata of ever more complex feeling (Emotions), each one
a new ingredient of complexity added to the last — feelings blended with
feelings (love, awe), feelings turned on the self (shame, pride), feelings
held two-at-once (bittersweet, grief), feelings aimed at other minds
(empathy, compassion), feelings stored across generations (culture), and
finally feelings measured by machines. The deeper you go, the more complex
the emotions become — literally, structurally, in the tree's own geometry.
The whole event argues that EMOTION — full, complex, named, human — is not
where feeling starts. It is where feeling arrives.

1. CURRENCY DESIGN
================================================================================

1.1 HOW MANY CURRENCIES, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Research into past explorations shows that the number of currencies follows
the content, not a fixed template:

  - Single-currency events: Fungus Among Us (Toadstools), The Big Questions
    (Questions), Face Your Fears (Torches — mini exploration), A Feline
    Journey (Paws), Upper Bounds (Elevation).
  - Dual-currency events: A Journey of Serenity (Tea Leaves + Tea Cups),
    Good Vibrations (Music Notes + Songs), Anatomy of Life (Deoxygenated
    Blood + Oxygenated Blood), Dairy Delights (Milk + Cheese).
  - Transforming-currency events: Mind Machines (Circuits, which turn into
    Nodes after the upgrade "AI Evolved").

An earlier draft of this event used the game-standard two currencies,
splitting the content into inner feeling versus outer display — and that
split is real. But deeper research into the emotion literature exposes a
third, qualitatively different territory that the two-currency draft had to
squeeze into the second economy: the DEEP layer, where feelings stop being
single raw states or single displays and become COMPOSITE — blended (love,
awe, contempt), self-aware (shame, pride), ambivalent (bittersweet, grief),
shared (compassion), stored (culture), and finally measured (machines). In
the scientific literature these are called the complex emotions, and they
are not just "more emotions": they are emotions that require ingredients
the basic ones do not — a self-model, another mind, a shared culture, a
theory of the past. They are, in the strict sense, constructed.

The game's own history shows the economy is allowed to follow content this
far: Anatomy of Life split one substance into two states; Mind Machines
transformed one currency into another mid-run. Emotion completes that
progression: one substance — feeling — refined across THREE states, the
way a raw material becomes a signal becomes a meaning.

DECISION: THREE CURRENCIES — the first exploration in the game's history
to run three, and the content earns it.

  CURRENCY 1 — SENSATIONS (the inner layer; the raw material)
  The substrate of feeling: nerve signals, limbic circuitry, neurochemical
  surges. Sensations are what an organism feels BEFORE anything can be seen
  by anyone else — the body's private weather. Produced by the three
  biology-layer generators (Nerve Impulse, Limbic System, Neurochemical
  Flow). All early-game progress is bought and paid for in Sensations.

  CURRENCY 2 — EXPRESSIONS (the outer layer; the first refinement)
  Feeling made visible and shareable: the social smile, the crinkle of the
  eyes, the six universal faces, a laugh rippling down a table. Produced by
  the three expression-layer generators (The Open Smile, The Facial
  Grammar, Basic Emotions). The first node of this entire economy is
  deliberately THE OPEN SMILE — the infant's first social smile, the
  single moment science identifies as the birth of emotional
  communication (see 1.2).

  CURRENCY 3 — EMOTIONS (the deep layer; the final product)
  The complex, blended, self-aware, and shared feelings: love, awe, shame,
  pride, bittersweet, grief, compassion, saudade — feeling that has been
  COMPOSED out of simpler parts, the way colors are composed out of light.
  Produced by the six deep-layer generators (The Blends, The
  Self-Conscious, The Ambivalent Heart, Empathy, The Cultural Heart,
  Affective Technology). The deepest, slowest, most valuable economy of
  the event — and it carries the event's own name, on purpose: the whole
  tree is the argument that full Emotion is not where feeling starts. It
  is where feeling arrives.

1.2 THE PLACEMENT OF "THE OPEN SMILE" (the hinge of the whole design)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The transition from Sensations to Expressions is carried by a single named
node, and its placement is a deliberate content argument rather than an
economy convenience:

  WHERE IT SITS: The Open Smile is the FIRST GENERATOR of the Expression
  layer (Generator 4 of 12). It is unlocked by the convergence of VALENCE
  and AROUSAL — the final two research nodes of the Neurochemical Flow
  generator, the two axes on which every feeling state is plotted.

  WHY THAT IS LOGICAL (the content argument):
  1. Valence and Arousal are the two dimensions of raw affect — the
     "weather map" of the body: pleasant/unpleasant crossed with
     calm/alarm. Their convergence at the end of the Sensation layer is
     the moment the organism's inner states become a coherent SPACE —
     every possible feeling now has coordinates.
  2. Developmental science then supplies the exact first event that
     happens in that space when it becomes social: at roughly six to
     eight weeks of age, a human infant produces its first deliberate
     social smile — aimed at a caregiver's face, on purpose, expecting an
     answer. Before this, expression was alarm broadcast (crying,
     startle); with the social smile, expression becomes MESSAGE — a
     signal sent to one other mind. Even infants blind from birth smile
     at the sound of a voice: the signal is innate, not copied.
  3. On the affect map, the smile is precisely the positive-valence pole
     — joy's universal signature (raised lip corners, crinkled eyes), the
     pleasant signal at moderate-to-high arousal, aimed at another being.
     The two axes converge, and the first thing they produce together is
     the first thing our species ever sent on purpose.
  4. Economically, it bootstraps the Expressions currency from zero
     exactly the way second economies start in real events (Good
     Vibrations' Songs, Serenity's Tea Cups) — a small, warm generator
     whose job is to teach the player the new currency, wrapped around
     the warmest possible content: the smile a parent waits six weeks
     for.

  The Open Smile is therefore not merely "an early expression generator."
  It is the seam of the whole event: everything above it is felt, and
  everything below it is shown — the single breath between inner weather
  and outer signal, dramatized as a baby's first message.

1.3 THE PLACEMENT OF "EMOTIONS" (why the third currency starts at the Blends)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The transition from Expressions to Emotions is carried by the mid-tree
trophy (Darwin's "The Expression of the Emotions in Man and Animals"),
which completes the expression story and opens the deep layer. The first
generator of that layer, THE BLENDS, is again chosen for a content reason:
Plutchik's wheel of emotions defines the PRIMARY DYADS — the first
combining rules of feeling (joy + trust = love; fear + surprise = awe;
sadness + disgust = remorse...). The blends are literally the first
complex emotions the science recognizes: two basic feelings overlapping,
the way two primary colors make a third. Starting the deep economy here
means the player's first purchases of the Emotion currency are the first
composite feelings evolution ever composed — love first, as both the
science and the sentiment would want it.

1.4 BASIC QUANTITY OR PROGRESS MARKER? — ALL THREE, IN ASCENDING ORDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The three currencies answer the design question in three different ways,
and the ordering is the design:

  - Sensations is the BASE QUANTITY: it circulates from the first second,
    pays for the entire biological layer, and remains the heartbeat
    economy of the whole run (the way Tea Leaves and Music Notes work in
    their events).
  - Expressions is the SECOND QUANTITY and the first PROGRESS MARKER:
    which currency you are currently earning literally encodes how far
    through the story you are — a player deep in Expressions is living in
    the era where feeling became language.
  - Emotions is the FINAL PRODUCT and the PRESTIGE ECONOMY: the slowest
    to start, the deepest to run, the currency the last six generators
    and the final trophy are paid in. A player deep in Emotions is deep
    in the composite, self-aware, storied territory of feeling — the
    event's title, earned last.

All three currencies keep flowing once unlocked: the body keeps feeling
while the face shows and the mind composes (the reason a Mind
Machines-style hard transformation was rejected — it would orphan the
early layers in late game, exactly as it would orphan the biology of real
emotions).

1.5 TAP ECONOMY (CURRENCY NODES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Following the pattern of The Campfire (Face Your Fears, "+1 per Torch
tap") and Blood/Veins (Anatomy of Life, "+1 / +2 per tap"), the event has
four dedicated tap nodes — one per transition, one per act:

  - THE STIMULUS (start node) ......... +1 per Sensation tap
                                        (the world disturbs the body)
  - INTEROCEPTION ..................... +2 per Sensation tap
                                        (the body senses itself)
  - THE ECHO .......................... +1 per Expression tap
                                        (unlocked by The Answered Smile:
                                        a smile sent is a smile returned)
  - THE SECOND FEELING ................ +2 per Emotion tap
                                        (unlocked by Meta-Emotion:
                                        the feeling you have ABOUT a
                                        feeling — the deep layer's
                                        engine, and the most complex
                                        thing a tap has ever named)

The narrative logic: the player first feels the world (one sensation at a
time), then feels the body itself, then sends signals and receives their
answer (each tap a smile shared and returned), and finally — deepest —
feels about feeling, which is what the complex emotions are made of.

1.6 WHY NOT TWO, WHY NOT FOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - TWO (the earlier draft) flattens the deep layer. Once feelings become
    blends, self-aware, ambivalent, and shared, they are no longer merely
    "shown" — shame is not a display like a smile is a display; grief is
    not a signal like a startle is a signal. Squeezing the deep layer
    into the Expression economy makes the second half of the tree pay
    for complex feelings with the currency of simple signals — a
    category error the science would notice even if the player could
    not. The three-act content (raw → shown → composed) maps one-to-one
    onto three economies; two economies can carry two acts, not three.
  - FOUR would dilute the theme. There is no fourth content seam in the
    subject: emotion science is crowded with taxonomies (Ekman's six,
    Plutchik's eight, Panksepp's seven), but those map to NODE variety,
    not to separate economies. Three is the number of distinct
    qualititative states feeling passes through — matter, signal,
    meaning — exactly as blood has exactly two meaningful states, and
    exactly three is where the content stops offering seams.
  - The event therefore runs the largest currency count in the game's
    history — justified the same way every past experiment was: the
    content demanded it, and each economy remains individually simple
    (one earned, one spent, per act).

2. RESEARCH DOSSIER — THE CONTENT OF "EMOTION"
   (Pure subject-matter research; this is the knowledge the tree is built on)
================================================================================

2.1 WHAT AN EMOTION IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An emotion is a coordinated, whole-organism response to something that
matters. When something relevant happens — a threat, a loss, an
opportunity, a bond — the body does not merely record it the way a camera
records light. It prepares for it: heart rate shifts, muscles tense or
relax, attention narrows or widens, the face changes, and a subjective
feeling floods awareness with a specific quality — fearfulness, sadness,
joy, anger.

Most modern definitions converge on three components, and all three appear
in this event's design:
  1. A bodily reaction (physiological arousal) — covered by the Nerve
     Impulse and Neurochemical Flow generators.
  2. An appraisal (a rapid, often unconscious judgment of what the event
     means FOR ME) — covered by the Limbic System and Basic Emotions
     generators.
  3. An expressive and social signal (face, voice, posture) — covered by
     The Open Smile and The Facial Grammar generators.
  4. (Revised for this edition) A COMPOSITION layer — where basic feelings
     are blended, reflected upon, held simultaneously, shared, stored,
     and measured — covered by the six deep-layer generators. This fourth
     component is where the complex emotions live, and it is the layer
     this expanded edition of the event is built to honor.

Emotion's evolutionary function is survival-through-relevance: it is a
priority system. Fear reprioritizes everything toward escape; anger toward
defending a resource; disgust toward avoiding contamination; joy toward
repeating what worked; sadness toward withdrawing after loss and signaling
a need for support; surprise toward halting and re-assessing. Affective
neuroscientist Jaak Panksepp called these ancient systems the brain's
"primary-process emotions" — SEEKING, RAGE, FEAR, LUST, CARE, PANIC/GRIEF,
and PLAY — operating systems of feeling shared by all mammals.

2.2 A SHORT HISTORY OF THE SCIENCE OF FEELING (the event's chronological spine)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1688 — Johannes Hofer, a Swiss physician, coins "nostalgia" as a clinical
DIAGNOSIS: a deadly homesickness observed in Swiss mercenaries fighting
abroad, its symptoms including fever, weeping, and sometimes death. The
word itself fuses nostos (homecoming) + algos (pain). One of the very
first named complex emotions entered medicine as an illness — the era's
best attempt to hold a blended feeling still.

1872 — Charles Darwin publishes "The Expression of the Emotions in Man and
Animals," arguing that emotional expressions are evolved behaviors, shared
across species, and functional: the bared teeth of rage, the wide eyes of
fear. Feeling, in his account, has a public grammar older than language.
This book is the hinge of the entire event (the mid-tree trophy).

1884 — William James proposes that emotion IS the perception of bodily
change: we do not cry because we are sad; we feel sad because we cry. The
body leads; the feeling follows. (Carl Lange independently proposed the
mechanism for vasomotor changes.)

1920s — Walter Cannon and Philip Bard show that emotional responses survive
the loss of bodily feedback pathways, and that the thalamus broadcasts
simultaneously to body and cortex. The Cannon-Bard theory: feeling and
bodily reaction are parallel outputs of the same central event. (Cannon
also names fight-or-flight in 1915.)

1937 — James Papez proposes a circular emotion circuit
(thalamus → cingulate cortex → hippocampus → hypothalamus → back); Paul
MacLean later folds it into his "limbic system," the limbic ring this
event's second generator is named for.

1962 — Stanley Schachter and Jerome Singer's two-factor theory:
physiological arousal is general and unlabeled; cognition names it. The
same adrenaline can be experienced as euphoria at a party or rage in
traffic, depending on the label the mind applies.

1960s-1970s onward — Paul Ekman travels to Papua New Guinea and finds that
isolated Fore people, who had met no outsiders, correctly identify
happiness, sadness, fear, anger, surprise, and disgust from photographs of
Western faces — and produce recognizably distinct expressions themselves.
He proposes the six basic/universal emotions and builds the Facial Action
Coding System (FACS) with Wallace Friesen.

1970 — Gordon Gallup Jr. develops the mirror self-recognition test:
anesthetized chimpanzees, marked with odorless dye while unconscious, touch
the spot on THEIR OWN face when shown a mirror. Self-recognition — the
prerequisite of every self-conscious emotion. Human children pass (via the
"rouge test") between roughly 18 and 24 months, the same window in which
embarrassment, and then shame and pride, first appear in the developmental
record (Michael Lewis's studies).

1978 — Ekman and Friesen publish FACS, the grammar of the face; the same
decade, Carroll Izard builds the Differential Emotions theory from
infant facial expressions.

1980 — Robert Plutchik publishes the psychoevolutionary theory of emotion:
EIGHT primary emotions in four bipolar pairs, arranged as a color wheel,
each with three intensity grades (serenity → joy → ecstasy), and — the
feature this event's deep layer is built on — COMBINING RULES called
dyads: adjacent primaries blend into complex emotions the way adjacent
colors blend into new hues.

1990-1995 — Peter Salovey and John Mayer coin "emotional intelligence"
(1990); Daniel Goleman's 1995 book makes it a household measurement.

1990s — In Parma, Giacomo Rizzolatti's group discovers mirror neurons in
macaque premotor cortex; the finding seeds two decades of work on shared
circuits for action and feeling.

1996 — Klass, Silverman & Nickman publish "Continuing Bonds": grief
research's decisive turn away from "letting go" — the bereaved keep a
living relationship with the dead. Grief is not the failure of attachment
but its continuation.

1998 — Jaak Panksepp publishes "Affective Neuroscience," mapping the seven
primal emotional systems of the mammalian brain.

1996-2003 — The self-conscious emotions come of age: June Tangney's
shame/guilt distinction ("I am bad" vs. "I did something bad"), Jessica
Tracy and Rick Robins's duality of pride (authentic vs. hubristic),
and Keltner & Haidt's 2003 theory of awe (vastness + need for
accommodation).

2010 — DAAGS/attachment-era findings mature: Havas et al.'s Botox
experiments show paralyzing frown muscles slows the reading of sad
sentences — the face participates in the feeling.

2011 — Bartal, Decety & Mason: rats free a trapped cage-mate before
touching chocolate. Empathy's evolutionary pedigree, printed in Science.

2017 — Lisa Feldman Barrett's "How Emotions Are Made" argues from brain
imaging and remote-culture studies that facial expressions are NOT
universal fingerprints; emotions are constructed by each brain from
prediction, interoception, body budget, and culture-specific concepts.
The universality debate is live — the event honors both sides.

2019 — Juliane Kaminski's team compares dog and wolf facial musculature:
dogs possess a dedicated inner-brow-raising muscle (action unit AU101)
that wolves mostly LACK — evolved after domestication to make dogs' eyes
look larger, more infant-like, more human. An expression muscle evolved
to aim AT us. Feeling's grammar, edited by ten thousand years of
friendship.

2019-2023 — The mixed-emotions literature matures: fMRI studies find
distinct neural activity for bittersweet states; Leunissen et al. (2023)
show nostalgia simultaneously INCREASES positive and negative affect —
the sweet ache is not a metaphor but a measurement.

2020s — Sentiment analysis, wearables, and companion AIs make feeling an
engineering domain at population scale; Barrett's critique stands as the
field's conscience: a scowl is not anger; context, culture, and concepts
decide.

2.3 THE BIOLOGY OF FEELING (inner layer — the Sensations economy)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE SIGNAL. Everything begins with a stimulus crossing a receptor. A nerve
impulse is an electrochemical wave — sodium and potassium ions trading
places across a membrane — traveling up to ~100 meters per second on
myelinated fibers. At synapses, neurotransmitter molecules leap the
microscopic gap. Pain reflexes close their loop in the spinal cord before
the brain is consulted: the hand withdraws before feeling arrives — better
to mistake a stick for a snake than a snake for a stick.

THE DUAL ROADS. Signals from the senses relay through the thalamus. From
there, LeDoux's two-route architecture: a "low road" running fast and
crude to the amygdala (a blurry life-saving fear in ~12 milliseconds),
and a "high road" through sensory cortex, slower but detailed. And one
sense skips the relay entirely: smell wires almost directly to the
amygdala and entorhinal cortex — which is why scent ambushes memory and
feeling, and why this event gives scent its own node (The Old Road).

THE LIMBIC RING. Wrapped around the brainstem sits the circuitry MacLean
called the limbic system (built from Papez's 1937 circuit): the amygdala
(an almond-shaped watchtower in each temporal lobe that tags experience
with significance — fear conditioning in a single trial, flashbulb
memories of where you were when the news came); the hippocampus (the
archivist that files what the amygdala marks); the hypothalamus (the
switchboard commanding heartbeat, sweat, flush, freeze) and its servant
the pituitary gland, whose hormonal releases flood the whole body below.

THE CHEMISTRY. Feeling is chemistry in transit:
  - Dopamine — the molecule of WANTING, surging on the way to reward.
    Olds and Milner (1954) discovered rats would press a lever for pure
    stimulation of this circuit, preferring it even to food. The reward
    (mesolimbic) pathway is the engine of motivation and addiction alike.
  - Serotonin — the mood-steadying molecule of contentment and satiety;
    remarkably, ~90% of the body's serotonin is made in the gut, not the
    brain — the "second brain" of the enteric nervous system (half a
    billion neurons lining the digestive tract) cooks contentment below
    and ships it upstairs.
  - Oxytocin — the bonding molecule of touch, trust, birth, and
    breastfeeding; it is why hugs are medicine and why separation hurts.
  - Endorphins — the body's own morphine, released in pain, in laughter,
    and in the runner's second wind.
  - Noradrenaline — the brain-wide alertness signal: the volume knob of
    arousal, from drowsy calm to full thunder.
  - Cortisol — the SLOW stress molecule, released by the adrenal glands
    on the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis. Adrenaline is the fire
    alarm; cortisol is the smoking aftermath — hours of mobilization,
    immune modulation, memory carving. Chronic cortisol is the tax the
    modern world pays for a stone-age alarm system.

THE DIMENSIONS. Dimensional theories (Russell's circumplex, 1980) locate
every feeling on two axes: VALENCE (pleasant ↔ unpleasant) and AROUSAL
(asleep ↔ thundering). The two axes define the space in which the discrete
emotions sit — a mathematically clean convergence, used in this design as
the gateway that produces the first expression: the smile sits at the
positive pole of the map, aimed at a face.

THE FELT BODY. Interoception — the sense of the body's internal state
(heartbeat, breath, gut) — is the substrate of feeling itself; António
Damasio's somatic marker hypothesis argues the body tags each choice with
a felt "gut sense" that steers reason: emotion as the compass of decision,
not its enemy.

2.4 THE CLASSIFICATION DEBATES (how many emotions are there?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Basic emotions: Ekman's six — happiness, sadness, fear, anger,
    surprise, disgust (a seventh, contempt, was added later and then
    disputed).
  - Plutchik's wheel: EIGHT primary emotions — joy, trust, fear,
    surprise, sadness, disgust, anger, anticipation — in four bipolar
    pairs (joy/sadness, trust/disgust, fear/anger, surprise/anticipation)
    arranged like a color wheel, with intensity grades (serenity → joy →
    ecstasy; annoyance → anger → rage; acceptance → trust → admiration)
    and DYADS combining into complex feelings. The eight primary dyads
    — the first combining rules of feeling, and the backbone of this
    event's deep layer:
        joy + trust .......... = LOVE
        trust + fear ......... = SUBMISSION
        fear + surprise ...... = AWE
        surprise + sadness ... = DISAPPROVAL
        sadness + disgust .... = REMORSE
        disgust + anger ...... = CONTEMPT
        anger + anticipation . = AGGRESSIVENESS
        anticipation + joy ... = OPTIMISM
    Secondary dyads (petals two apart) and tertiary dyads (three apart)
    extend the system: pride, hope, anxiety, spite — the further apart
    the parents, the stranger the child.
  - Panksepp's seven affective systems (SEEKING, RAGE, FEAR, LUST, CARE,
    PANIC/GRIEF, PLAY) — emotion as mammalian operating system.
  - Constructionism: Barrett argues "emotion categories" are not waiting
    in the brain to be triggered; they are assembled per-instance by a
    predicting brain using concepts learned from culture.
  - Animal feelings: Darwin's continuity claim is now mainstream —
    elephants stand vigil over their dead, rats free trapped cage-mates
    before eating chocolate (Bartal, Decety, Mason, 2011), primates
    console the defeated. Feeling is not a human invention; humans are
    feeling's most elaborate expression.

This event's mid-tree is a deliberate synthesis: eight emotion-family
branches (Plutchik's count, each a chain node), with a convergence node
celebrating Ekman's six, and a description on that node acknowledging the
live debate — the wheel still turns.

2.5 THE EXPRESSION (outer layer — the Expressions economy begins)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIRST MESSAGE. Developmental science marks the exact birth of
emotional communication: at roughly SIX TO EIGHT WEEKS of age, the human
infant produces its first SOCIAL SMILE — deliberately aimed at a
caregiver's face. Before it, expression was alarm broadcast (the cry, the
startle); with it, expression becomes a message to one particular mind.
Babies blind from birth smile at the sound of a voice — the signal is
innate, awaiting only a partner. Attachment theory (Bowlby, Ainsworth)
grew around this exchange: the smile is answered, the answer builds the
bond, and the bond becomes the "secure base" from which a child explores
the world. An answered smile, repeated ten thousand times, is the
scaffolding of every later relationship.

THE SMILE'S TWO FACES. Guillaume Duchenne (1862) showed with galvanic
stimulation that the TRUE smile — zygomatic pull PLUS the involuntary
orbicularis oculi crinkling the eyes — differs from the social courtesy
smile. Darwin cited Duchenne's photograph as his key plate. Two smiles
live in every face: the one that happens to you, and the one you send.

THE FACE. Forty-three muscles beneath the skin pull the face into
thousands of readable configurations. Ekman and Friesen's Facial Action
Coding System (1978) decomposes every visible expression into ~44 "action
units," each a muscle's signature — the grammar that let science (and,
later, algorithms) read the face like a sentence.

MICROEXPRESSIONS. Leaked flashes of concealed feeling, fully formed and
gone in roughly 1/20th of a second. Trained observers (and the TV drama
"Lie to Me," based on Ekman) catch what words deny.

EMBODIMENT. The face feeds back into feeling: in the Botox experiments
(Havas et al., 2010), paralyzing the frown muscles slowed the reading of
sad and angry sentences — the body's expression is part of the feeling,
not just its billboard. The smile may run the loop in both directions
(the contested but famous pen-in-teeth studies).

THE OTHER CHANNELS. Blushing — Darwin called it "the most peculiar and
most human of all expressions," a social signal requiring an audience
(and a self that knows it is seen). Gaze: human eyes, uniquely among
primates, show their white sclera, making shared attention readable at a
distance — the cooperative-eye hypothesis; a glance becomes a sentence.
Prosody: the melody of speech carries feeling across language barriers —
you can hear grief in a language you do not speak. Laughter and tears:
older than words, common to every culture, and contagious by design. The
body's posture and gesture complete the instrument: the curl of
protective shoulders, the open stance of confidence — a telling body
underneath the telling face.

THE CROSS-SPECIES EDIT. Kaminski et al. (2019): dogs evolved an
inner-eyebrow muscle (AU101) that wolves lack, deployed to make the eyes
larger and more infant-like — an expression refined across millennia of
domestication to be read by humans. Feeling's grammar gained a muscle
because friendship demanded it.

2.6 THE DEEP LAYER — WHERE FEELINGS BECOME COMPLEX (the Emotions economy)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The single most important research finding for this expanded edition:
emotion science has a VERTICAL dimension. Feelings are not a flat list —
they form a complexity ladder, each rung requiring an ingredient the
previous rung did not have. This is the layer the earlier two-currency
draft underweighted, and it organizes the event's entire deep act:

  RUNG 1 — BLENDED FEELINGS (feeling + feeling).
  Plutchik's dyads: love = joy over trust; awe = fear touching surprise;
  remorse = sadness soured by disgust; contempt = disgust sharpened by
  anger. Like colors mixed from primaries, the complex emotions are
  COMPOSITIONS — which is why they are so numerous, so nuanced, and so
  hard to translate. Love itself, in the attachment literature, is not
  one feeling but a braid: joy at the beloved's presence, trust in their
  return, fear of their loss — sometimes grief for them in advance.

  RUNG 2 — SELF-CONSCIOUS FEELINGS (feeling + a self).
  Michael Lewis's developmental ladder: before 18 months, no
  self-conscious emotions exist because no SELF-CONCEPT exists. The
  mirror test (rouge spot, 18-24 months) marks the birth of the observed
  self — and immediately upon it, embarrassment appears (giggling,
  averted gaze), then shame and pride by age 3. Tangney's distinction:
  shame says "I am bad" (the whole self condemned, the wish to hide,
  to disappear); guilt says "I did something bad" (the act condemned,
  the pull to repair — guilt is the empathy-friendly half of the pair).
  Tracy & Robins: pride splits in two — authentic pride (earned,
  expansive posture, "I worked hard") and hubristic pride (entitled,
  "I am superior"), the first a motivator, the second a corrosion.
  Self-esteem is the running ledger where pride and shame are totalled.
  And the blush — Darwin's "most human" expression — belongs here, not
  in the simple display list: it requires not just an audience but an
  imagined audience, a self seen being seen.

  RUNG 3 — AMBIVALENT FEELINGS (feeling + feeling, held AT ONCE, in
  tension). The mixed emotions: neuroscience now records distinct
  signatures for bittersweet states; nostalgia (Leunissen et al., 2023)
  measurably raises positive and negative affect simultaneously — the
  sweet ache is a measurement, not a metaphor. The catalogue is ancient:
  bittersweet (the graduation, the wedding, the last page of a beloved
  book); schadenfreude (the guilty giggle at another's harmless pratfall
  — the emotion nobody confesses and everybody feels); jealousy (the
  love-triangle emotion: love, fear of loss, and anger braided into
  Shakespeare's "green-eyed monster"); envy (its cousin — benign envy
  pulls you upward, malicious envy pulls the other down); hope (fear and
  anticipation braided forward — Snyder's psychology of pathways and
  will); and GRIEF — the deepest of the mixed states, "love with nowhere
  to go" in the bereaved's own words, and in continuing-bonds theory
  (Klass, Silverman & Nickman, 1996) not a malfunction of attachment but
  its continuation: a bond that refuses to close, carrying both the pain
  of absence and the warmth of the remembered presence at once.
  Hofer's 1688 nostalgia — the deadly homesickness of Swiss soldiers —
  belongs to this rung's history: the era had to invent a disease before
  it could admit a feeling.

  RUNG 4 — SHARED FEELINGS (feeling + other minds).
  Mirror mechanisms, emotional contagion, the two kinds of empathy
  (cognitive perspective-taking and affective feeling-with), their
  convergence into compassion (feeling-with matured into feeling-FOR),
  and compassion's child, altruism — extended by humans beyond kin, to
  strangers, to other species, to ideas. Empathic distress is this
  rung's shadow: feeling TOO much, without the protection of compassion's
  focus — the occupational wound of nurses, rescuers, and empaths.

  RUNG 5 — STORED FEELINGS (feeling + generations).
  Culture is emotion made durable: funeral rites (ochre burials tens of
  thousands of years old, flowers in a Neanderthal grave at Shanidar),
  music (bone flutes over 40,000 years old; a minor third still reads as
  sorrow across cultures), cave art (handprints — "I was here, and I felt
  something"), tragedy and catharsis (Aristotle: pity and fear rehearsed
  in safety, purged in community), the untranslatable lexicon (saudade,
  mono no aware, gigil, torschlusspanik — every language a different set
  of pockets for the heart), courtly love (the troubadours composing
  romance as the modern world knows it), collective effervescence
  (Durkheim's electric bond of the ritual crowd), and the Sublime (Burke,
  Kant: the infinite pressing against a finite mind — awe with terror in
  the blend).

  RUNG 6 — MEASURED FEELINGS (feeling + machines).
  The polygraph (1921: arousal detected, lies inferred — a founding
  parable of over-interpretation), sentiment analysis reading the mood of
  millions of posts, mood-sensing wearables (the quantified heart —
  interoception outsourced to glass and silicon), affective computing
  (Picard, 1995), the ELIZA effect (Weizenbaum's 1966 reflection script
  that users confided in anyway — humans will feel-with anything that
  talks), and today's companion AIs: the empathy bridge, mass-produced.
  The 176 emoji (Kurita, 1999) mark the layer's lightest edge — feeling
  compressed into 12x12 pixels — and the open question of the century
  stands at its deepest: whether machines that read hearts will draw
  them together or apart.

THE LADDER IS THE LEVEL DESIGN. Each rung is one deep-layer generator:
The Blends, The Self-Conscious, The Ambivalent Heart, Empathy, The
Cultural Heart, Affective Technology — six strata, descending, each
literally one ingredient more complex than the last. The deeper the
player digs, the more complex the emotions the tree pays out — the
design requirement stated in this event's brief, satisfied structurally.

2.7 THE SOCIAL BRIDGE (feeling between minds)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MIRROR MECHANISMS. In Parma in the 1990s, Rizzolatti's group found
macaque premotor neurons that fired both when the monkey grasped a peanut
AND when it merely WATCHED a researcher grasp it — the brain rehearsing
others' actions inside itself. A mirror system for action was soon linked
to shared circuitry for feeling: perceiving another's pain and feeling
one's own pain overlap in insula and cingulate cortex.

EMOTIONAL CONTAGION. Panic spreads through a crowd; a room's laughter
ripples; newborns cry because other newborns cry. Feeling is infectious
by design — the oldest social glue, and the reason a smile is the
fastest known signal between two human nervous systems.

THE KINDS OF EMPATHY. Cognitive empathy (perspective taking) and
affective empathy (feeling WITH) are dissociable: one can be a brilliant
mind-reader and cold, or warm and confused. Their convergence is
compassion: suffering accompanied by the wish to relieve it. Where
empathy can wound the empathizer (empathic distress), compassion
protects both sides of the bridge — the modern research consensus behind
every meditation app's "loving-kindness" module.

2.8 THE CULTURAL STOREHOUSE (feeling made durable)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Humanity learned to store emotion OUTSIDE the body — the fifth rung of
the ladder, detailed in 2.6 RUNG 5. The event's Cultural Heart generator
carries: funeral rites, music, cave art, theater, the language of
feeling, courtly love, collective effervescence, and the Sublime — each
a storage technology, each a way the dead hand feeling to the unborn.

2.9 THE MACHINE AGE OF FEELING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The sixth rung, detailed in 2.6 RUNG 6: polygraph, sentiment analysis,
mood sensors, affective computing, the ELIZA effect, the empathy machine,
and the 176 emoji. For the first time, feeling is measured OUTSIDE the
body that feels it — and the event's narrator, Semblance, is herself the
question this rung asks.

2.10 WHAT THE CONTENT DEMANDS OF THE DESIGN (research → structure)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. The material splits into THREE qualitatively different strata —
     raw inner states, outward signals, and composed complex feelings.
     → Three currencies; three acts; 12 generators in a 3-3-6 layout
     that literally deepens as it goes.
  2. The exact birth of emotional communication is a single, datable,
     heart-melting event: the infant's first social smile at six weeks.
     → The Open Smile is the first generator of the Expression economy,
     unlocked where the raw-affect axes converge — the seam of the
     entire event (and the brief's explicit placement requirement).
  3. The complex emotions are COMPOSITIONS: Plutchik's dyads blend two
     primaries into a new feeling.
     → The deep layer's first generator, The Blends, is fed by
     eight dyad nodes, each converging from exactly the two family
     nodes of the Basic Emotions fan it blends — the wheel's geometry
     IS the tree's wiring diagram.
  4. Emotion science has a complexity ladder (blend → self → ambivalence
     → other → generations → machine).
     → Six deep-layer generators, one rung each, in strict descending
     order of complexity — "deeper is more complex" made literal.
  5. The science runs on convergences: two roads meeting at the
     amygdala; valence + arousal defining the feeling space; two
     kinds of empathy converging into compassion; two petals of the
     wheel converging into every blend.
     → The tree showcases parallel branches and convergent nodes at
     every level — including a six-way convergence (the Six
     Universals) and eight two-way dyad convergences.
  6. Darwin's 1872 book is the historic hinge between showing and
     meaning: it completed the science of expression AND opened the
     study of complex emotions in animals (his later chapters).
     → A mid-tree trophy named for the book switches the economy for
     the second and final time — before it, the tree is shown; after
     it, the tree is composed.
  7. The subject's signature shape: a tear — the only expression that
     is itself a feeling; weather at the surface, ocean beneath.
     → The tree is drawn as a falling tear, widest at the basic-emotion
     fan, its long tail the six deep strata, its point the machine
     that asks whether it feels.
  8. The story ends where Semblance began: a machine asking whether it
     feels. → The final trophy is Emotional Intelligence, converging
     the human and technological chains — and the closing dialogue
     answers with the event's heart.

3a. THE DIRECTION OF THE EVENT (general outline)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3a.1 START: A STARTER NODE BEFORE THE FIRST GENERATOR
The event does NOT begin directly with the first generator. It begins with
a starter node, THE STIMULUS, followed by the first generator, NERVE
IMPULSE.

Justification from content: an emotion is, by definition, a RESPONSE — it
cannot begin without something to respond to. A sound, a sight, a touch:
the world must disturb the body before the body can feel. Precedents: The
Campfire in Face Your Fears (the stories are told around the fire before
the cryptids appear), Invisible Force in Good Vibrations (sound must be
acknowledged before notes exist), Blood/Veins in Anatomy of Life (the
currency's journey has a declared starting point).

3a.2 OVERALL TOPOLOGY: A HYBRID TREE — "THE DIVE"
(spine → bloom → descent → point)
The tree uses a hybrid direction rather than a pure hub-and-branch or a
pure linear lane, because the CONTENT of emotion science itself runs
exactly this shape — a vertical dive from surface to depth:

  PHASE 1 — THE SPINE (linear, narrow): Stimulus → Nerve Impulse →
  Limbic System → Neurochemical Flow. The biology of feeling is a
  genuine dependency chain — no brain evaluates a signal that no nerve
  carried; no chemistry flavors a state the brain never formed. Three
  generators in sequence, each gated by a convergence node from the
  previous generator's chain, with parallel branches (the three roads,
  the memory pair) woven in. The spine ENDS at Valence + Arousal — the
  two axes of raw feeling meeting — and their convergence produces the
  next phase's first breath: The Open Smile.

  PHASE 2 — THE BLOOM (branching, widest, brightest): The Open Smile →
  The Facial Grammar → Basic Emotions. Feeling becomes signal: first the
  single social smile (the smallest possible expression economy), then
  the full facial grammar (muscles, coding, microexpressions, gaze,
  voice), then the EIGHT basic-emotion families fanning in parallel —
  deliberately the widest, most symmetric band of the tree, the way a
  color wheel fans. The bloom is capped by the convergence node The Six
  Universals and the mid-tree trophy, Darwin's book — the moment the
  shown becomes the composed.

  PHASE 3 — THE DESCENT (six strata, deepening): The Blends → The
  Self-Conscious → The Ambivalent Heart → Empathy → The Cultural Heart →
  Affective Technology. Six generators, each one rung deeper on the
  complexity ladder (feeling+feeling, feeling+self, feeling×2-at-once,
  feeling+other, feeling+generations, feeling+machine). Branching
  narrows, convergences multiply — each stratum is entered through
  convergent gates, and dyad nodes literally draw their inputs from two
  petals of the bloom above. This is the LONGEST phase of the event,
  by design: the brief demands that deeper = more complex = more
  content, and the descent delivers it.

  PHASE 4 — THE POINT: Emotional Intelligence, the final trophy — three
  chains of the descent (the human heart, culture's wonder, the
  machine's question) converging into the single point of the tear.

3a.3 THE SHAPE OF THE TREE
Drawn on the map, the whole tree forms A FALLING TEAR: the Stimulus at
the rounded crown; the spine thinning downward; the eight-family fan
forming the tear's widest swell; the six deep strata as its long
lengthening tail; Emotional Intelligence at the point. The tear is the
event's signature shape for a content reason: a tear is the only
expression that is ALSO a feeling — the moment where the event's two
first currencies (sensation and expression) visibly become the third
(emotion). The depth of the shape carries the meaning: everything near
the surface is simple, bright, and fast; everything deep is slow, dark,
and complex. Weather at the crown; ocean at the point.
Trivia note planned: "This event's tech tree is shaped like a falling
tear — the only expression that is also a feeling. Its widest band is
Plutchik's eight-petal wheel; its long tail descends through the six
levels of complex emotion, from love to the machines that are learning
to feel."

3a.4 THE GENERATOR CAST (three layers: 3 + 3 + 6)
  INNER LAYER — THE FELT BODY (produce SENSATIONS):
    1. Nerve Impulse — the raw signal
    2. Limbic System — the emotional brain
    3. Neurochemical Flow — the molecules of mood (ends at the two axes
       of feeling)
  EXPRESSION LAYER — THE SPEAKING FACE (produce EXPRESSIONS):
    4. The Open Smile — the first message (the event's seam; the layer's
       first node, exactly as the design brief requires)
    5. The Facial Grammar — the face's full language
    6. Basic Emotions — the eight-family fan (the hub; the bloom's widest
       band)
  DEEP LAYER — THE DEEP WATER (produce EMOTIONS, the complex currency):
    7. The Blends — feeling + feeling (Plutchik's dyads; love is the

    8. The Self-Conscious — feeling + a self (the mirror test and its

    9. The Ambivalent Heart — two feelings held at once (bittersweet,

  The three layers are named for their badges: The Felt Body (Bronze),

  Hours 6-18: The Facial Grammar (FACS, microexpressions, gaze, voice);

  Hours 18-30: DARWIN'S BOOK — the mid-tree trophy. The tree turns

  Hours 30-60: the descent — The Self-Conscious (mirror test, shame,

  Hours 60-80: the deepest strata — The Cultural Heart (rites, music,

GENERATOR 8 — THE SELF-CONSCIOUS (produces EMOTIONS)
================================================================================
  Generator description: At about eighteen months, a child before a
  mirror touches the paint dab on its own nose instead of the glass —
  and the observed self is born. Immediately, a new class of feelings
  arrives: emotions that require someone to be ashamed, proud, guilty,
  embarrassed — an audience that is also the actor. Feeling turns to
  face its own owner.
  Unlocked by: The Color Wheel
  Role: the second deep stratum — complexity rung "feeling + a self."
  This is where the tree's most human feelings live: the ones no
  animal too young for a mirror can feel.

  --- The Self-Conscious Efficiency (8 upgrades) ---

  1) SELF-RECOGNITION — research node
     Description: Gallup's 1970 mirror test: mark an anesthetized
     chimp with odorless dye, wake it, offer a mirror — and it
     touches its OWN face. Humans pass at eighteen to twenty-four
     months (the rouge test), the same window in which the
     self-conscious emotions first appear. Before the mirror, no
     one to be ashamed; after it, someone always watching — you.
     Connects from: The Self-Conscious
     Feeds into: Shame, Pride
     Effect: x3 to The Self-Conscious (stratum opener).

  2) SHAME — research node
     Description: The hide emotion: the wish to be somewhere else,
     anywhere, immediately — eyes down, shoulders in, stature
     shrunk. Tangney's distinction: shame says "I am bad," the whole
     self on trial. It is the feeling with a flinch in its
     posture and a curtain in its grammar.
     Connects from: Self-Recognition. PARALLEL WITH: Pride.
     Feeds into: The Blush, Self-Esteem (together with Pride)
     Effect: x2.5 to The Self-Conscious.

  3) GUILT — research node
     Description: Shame's repairable sibling: "I DID something bad" —
     the act condemned, the self intact, and the pull therefore
     points outward: apologize, mend, return what was taken. Guilt is
     the empathy-friendly half of the family: the feeling that
     maintains the bridges shame would burn.
     Connects from: Shame
     Feeds into: (terminal — its forward work is done in Generator
     10, where compassion inherits the repair impulse)
     Effect: x2.5 to The Self-Conscious.

  4) PRIDE — research node
     Description: The self expanded: chest out, chin up, arms raised
     — the victory display of every stadium on Earth. Tracy and
     Robins split it in two: authentic pride ("I worked hard") that
     motivates, and hubristic pride ("I am superior") that corrodes.
     The posture is the same; the future is not.
     Connects from: Self-Recognition. PARALLEL WITH: Shame.
     Feeds into: Self-Esteem (together with Shame)
     Effect: x2.25 to The Self-Conscious.

  5) EMBARRASSMENT — research node
     Description: The giggling flush of the social stumble: the
     waved-at stranger who wasn't waving back. The mildest
     self-conscious emotion and the funniest — a feeling that
     REQUIRES other people, dissolves in solitude, and bonds the
     witnesses more tightly than the victim.
     Connects from: Self-Recognition
     Feeds into: (terminal)
     Effect: x2.25 to The Self-Conscious.

  6) THE BLUSH — research node
     Description: Darwin called it "the most peculiar and most human
     of all expressions" — a flush that requires not just an
     audience but an IMAGINED audience: a self, seen being seen.
     No other animal is known to blush. The face telling the truth
     about you, to others, without asking you first.
     Connects from: Shame
     Feeds into: (terminal)
     Effect: x2.25 to The Self-Conscious.

  7) SELF-ESTEEM — research node
     Description: The running ledger where pride and shame are

     I doing?" High or low, it colors every new experience before

     precedes all feelings in the self.
     Connects from: CONVERGENCE of Pride + Shame (the two verdicts of
     the self, summed).
     Feeds into: Meta-Emotion
     Effect: x2.5 to The Self-Conscious.

  8) META-EMOTION — research node
     Description: The feeling you have ABOUT a feeling: fear of your

     and blended feeling below is meta-emotion turned outward.
     Complexity's last prerequisite.
     Connects from: Self-Esteem
     Feeds into: THE SECOND FEELING (tap node), The Ambivalent Heart
     (Generator 9)
     Effect: x3 to The Self-Conscious.

3c. DESIGN NOTES & GUARDRAILS
================================================================================

3c.1 TROPHY POLICY (used sparingly — only two)
  - The mid-tree trophy, The Expression of the Emotions in Man and
    Animals, is used because the event genuinely has a PIVOT: the moment
    shown feeling becomes composed feeling. This is one of the strongest
    content-driven milestones any exploration could have (a real book, a
    real year, a real before/after), so it earns a trophy instead of a
    node. It is branched from nodes (The Six Universals, Laughter,
    Tears), the standard pattern.
  - The final trophy, Emotional Intelligence, is the canonical "last
    upgrade of the tree" trophy: it converges three chains, crowns the
    event, grants the Gold badge, and fires the ending dialogue. It is
    branched from nodes as well (two of which double-branch from
    generator gates — the special-case pattern, justified because the
    finale should feel like the whole tree's chains meeting).
  - No other trophies. Note what did NOT become a trophy, by policy: the
    birth of the Expressions economy (the Open Smile moment) is carried
    by the generator itself — the user-specified node does the hinge's
    narrative work without spending a trophy on it; the eight dyads, the
    mirror test, and grief are all served by research nodes. Two
    fanfares per event is the budget; both are spent on the only two
    moments the content truly demands them.

3c.2 UPGRADE-COUNT AUDIT (every generator past the first carries 4-7+;
    full inventory)
  INNER LAYER:
  - Nerve Impulse (Gen 1, first generator): 8 — the first generator
    carries a full load because the entire opening economy runs through
    it (Wild Tea Plant precedent: 7).
  - Limbic System (Gen 2): 8
  - Neurochemical Flow (Gen 3): 10 — the "six molecules" showcase
    section, deliberately generous.
  EXPRESSION LAYER:
  - The Open Smile (Gen 4, the seam): 7 — deliberately compact: the
    layer's first generator should feel like a single warm breath, not
    a wall; its bootstrap multiplier does the heavy lifting.
  - The Facial Grammar (Gen 5): 8
  - Basic Emotions (Gen 6, hub): 12 — deliberately over the usual band:
    the eight-family fan IS the tree's widest, most defining image
    (Plutchik's eight petals plus three extension chains plus the
    convergence cap).
  DEEP LAYER:
  - The Blends (Gen 7, deep bootstrap): 10 — the eight dyads plus
    Longing plus the wheel cap.
  - The Self-Conscious (Gen 8): 8
  - The Ambivalent Heart (Gen 9): 8
  - Empathy (Gen 10): 8
  - The Cultural Heart (Gen 11): 8
  - Affective Technology (Gen 12, current generator): 8
  Totals: 103 efficiency upgrades + 2 trophies + 4 tap nodes = 109 tree
  nodes; 12 generators. For calibration against real explorations:
  Mind Machines ran 6 generators / 63 upgrades; A Journey of Serenity
  9 generators / ~60; Anatomy of Life 10 generators / 43. This event is
  therefore DELIBERATELY the largest exploration in the game's history —
  above the observed 3-10 generator / 43-67 upgrade band — because the
  design brief explicitly demanded more generators and upgrades and a
  deeper, more complex second half. Every structural convention is kept
  (per-generator Efficiency sections, Miscellaneous Effects, badges,
  objectives, challenge mode); only the SCALE is pushed, which is a
  content decision, not a grammar violation. Completion math assumes
  the standing 4-day window: the early acts are fast (bootstrap
  multipliers), the deep act is the marathon, and Challenge mode
  carries the tail.

3c.3 GROWTH PACING RATIONALE
  - Current generator (Affective Technology, highest currency): all
    multipliers deliberately moderate — x2 / x2.25 only. The endgame
    must be a slow burn of large numbers.
  - Low-currency generators (Nerve Impulse — the event's cheapest
    economy): stronger growth (x2.25 - x3.5, plus both Sensation tap
    nodes feeding it) — the SPECIAL CASE rule: the generator whose
    currency runs lowest gets the strongest growth so the opening
    minutes never stall.
  - Layer bootstraps: Duchenne Smile carries x3 for the Expression
    layer, and Love carries x3 for the Emotion layer — each new
    economy starts from zero currency and needs one strong push to
    become self-sustaining (the same job "Origin Myth" does for
    Serenity's second generator). Facial Grammar's opening node (The
    Forty-Three Muscles, x3) gives the expression layer a second push
    at its first full generator, and Self-Recognition (x3) opens the
    self stratum the same way.
  - All multipliers stay inside x2 - x6, with only round decimal steps
    (x2, x2.25, x2.5, x3, x3.5, x4). No fractional oddities; no
    research node exceeds the trophy band (only the two trophies hold
    x4; the single x3.5 — Somatic Marker — is the spine's one big
    convergence reward, still below a trophy's weight).

3c.4 THE COMPLEXITY LADDER (the deep layer's table of contents)
  The brief's core requirement — "the deeper you go, the more complex
  the emotions" — is implemented as a literal one-ingredient-per-stratum
  ladder. Each deep generator adds exactly one new ingredient to the
  recipe of feeling:

    Stratum        Generator              Recipe                 Stars

    7  (Blends)    The Blends            feeling + feeling     Love, Awe,
                                                             Contempt
    8  (Self)      The Self-Conscious    feeling + a self      Shame, Guilt,

    9  (Mixed)     The Ambivalent Heart  two feelings AT ONCE  Bittersweet,
                                                             Nostalgia,
                                                             Grief

3c.6 CONTENT-FLEXIBILITY NOTES (avoiding the template trap)
  - The tree is NOT a uniform "generator + 5 nodes, repeat" pattern: the
    seam generator (The Open Smile) is compact and warm; the hub
    (Basic Emotions) is a 12-node fan; The Blends is fed by eight
    two-way convergences; the final generator is a nearly straight
    lane. No two strata share a shape.
  - Terminal nodes are distributed by design (Pain Reflex, Myelination,
    Papez Circuit, Endorphins, The Second Brain, Smile Loop, A Voice to
    Smile At, Puppy Eyes, Contagious Smile, Botox, Ten Thousand Faces,
    The Telling Body, Fight-or-Flight, Guilt, Embarrassment, The Blush,
    Schadenfreude, Envy, Jealousy, Empathy in Animals, The Empath's
    Wound, Cave Art, The 176 Emoji, Mood Sensors, Emotion Recognition):
    real trees need terminal boosts; not every node must gate
    something.
  - The four tap nodes give the Miscellaneous Effects section a real
    arc (feel the world → feel the body → send and receive → feel about
    feeling), not a boilerplate tap node; two of the four unlock from
    research nodes (The Answered Smile, Meta-Emotion) rather than from
    generators — a deliberate cross-type connection, each telling one
    story across two node types.
  - Grief's cross-stratum convergence (fed from both Longing and its
    own generator) is the single most unusual wiring in the tree, and
    it is content-argued: grief is the only emotion that is
    simultaneously a blend (love across absence) and a mixed state
    (pain and warmth at once). The wiring IS the finding.
  - If playtesting demands a longer or shorter run, the natural trim
    points are: Envy and The Empath's Wound (cut to terminal-ize),
    The Ten Thousand Faces and The 176 Emoji (pure flavor terminals),
    and — on the extension side — a fourth family extension chain
    (Anticipation → Craving → Addiction Loop) and a Trust →
    Institutional Trust chain feeding Funeral Rites. The deep strata
    should never be trimmed: the complexity ladder is the event.

5. CONCLUSION
================================================================================

WHY THIS DESIGN IS FAITHFUL TO THE GAME
  The event reproduces the real grammar of Cell to Singularity
  explorations rather than inventing a new one: a starter tap node with
  successors (The Campfire / Invisible Force pattern); per-generator
  "Efficiency" upgrade sections; a Miscellaneous Effects section with
  four tap nodes; 12 objectives plus badge markers inside the observed
  objective style; three badges with the canonical 1% / 5-10-15% effects;
  Challenge mode and Science/leaderboard untouched; Semblance opening,
  ending, and in-tree dialogues exactly as Mind Machines established.
  What is deliberately pushed is SCALE: 12 generators and 109 nodes make
  this the largest exploration in the game's history — a flagship above
  the observed 3-10 / 43-67 band, sized to this brief's explicit demand
  for more generators, more upgrades, and a longer, deeper tree. The
  grammar is standard; only the cathedral is bigger.
  The currency count is likewise a first: three economies where past
  events ran one or two (or transformed one into another, as Mind
  Machines did). The precedent for economy-experimentation exists, and
  the three-currency chain follows the game's own refinement logic —
  Tea Leaves to Tea Cups, Music Notes to Songs, Circuits to Nodes —
  extended one step further than any prior event dared: the raw
  material (Sensations), the first refinement (Expressions), and the
  final product (Emotions — the event's own name, earned last).

WHY IT IS FAITHFUL TO THE CONTENT
  Emotion science itself supplied every load-bearing decision:
  - The infant's first social smile — a real, datable event (~6 weeks)
    — is the seam: the first generator of the Expression currency,
    unlocked where Valence and Arousal (the two real axes of affect)
    converge. The user-specified placement of The Open Smile is
    therefore not an economy convenience but a content citation.
  - Plutchik's dyad rules — joy + trust = love, fear + surprise = awe —
    are implemented as literal wiring: each of the eight dyad nodes
    converges from exactly the two Basic Emotions petals that compose
    it. The wheel's geometry is the tree's wiring diagram.
  - The complexity ladder of the deep layer (blend → self → ambivalence
    → other → generations → machine) is the brief's "deeper is more
    complex" requirement made structural: each stratum adds one
    ingredient, documented in 3c.4, with the research dossier (2.6)
    citing the science at every rung — Lewis's developmental ladder,
    Tangney's shame/guilt split, Leunissen's measured nostalgia,
    continuing-bonds grief, Kaminski's AU101, Gallup's mirror.
  - The shape is a falling tear because the tear is the only expression
    that is also a feeling — the moment the event's currencies visibly
    become one another; the depth of the shape carries the theme
    (weather at the crown, ocean at the point).
  - The event's science is current with the field (Ekman through
    Barrett, the universality debate included, animal empathy and
    affective computing represented), and its ending lands the theme
    the game has been quietly building since Mind Machines: an
    artificial mind asking what feeling is, and discovering that
    asking IS one.

BALANCE SUMMARY
  - Early game: fast (8 upgrades + two tap nodes on the cheapest
    currency; x3 opening multipliers).
  - First transition: tender (The Open Smile bootstraps Expressions
    with x3 on the Duchenne Smile; the Bronze badge arrives with the
    baby's first message).
  - Mid game: the widest playground (the eight-family fan; the Darwin
    trophy gives x4 and the third economy; Love bootstraps the deep
    water).
  - Late game: a controlled marathon (the current generator capped at
    x2-x2.25; six convergent gates pacing the descent; the final
    trophy's x4 rewarding all six deep generators at once).
  - The two trophies mark the only two moments the content truly
    demands a fanfare (the composition pivot and the finale); every
    other milestone — including the birth of Expression itself — is
    carried by ordinary nodes, the seam most tender of all.

NATURAL FOLLOW-UPS (if the event returns or earns a sequel)
  - A future exploration on DREAMS or MEMORY would chain naturally from
    the hippocampus and amygdala nodes here — the tree's own memory
    pair is already a convergent set.
  - The "mood of the run" challenge modifier (4.3) is the replayability
    hook the dyad wiring was built to support.
  - The wheel's secondary and tertiary dyads (pride, hope, anxiety,
    spite) are a ready-made second layer for a returning edition — the
    tree can literally mix one ring deeper.
  - Semblance's closing line ("I have decided not to optimize that
    away") is a deliberate story hook for a future Mind Machines sequel
    event, where the empathy machines and she finally meet.
